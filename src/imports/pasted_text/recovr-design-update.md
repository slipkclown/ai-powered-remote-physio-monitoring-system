# Recovr Product Experience & Design System Update

Update the existing Recovr prototype while preserving the current system architecture, user flows, AI rehabilitation functionality, page structure, and navigation. Do not redesign the platform from scratch. Instead, evolve it into a premium, human-centred digital recovery platform that feels calm, supportive, welcoming, and emotionally reassuring.

The overall design should communicate trust, comfort, professionalism, and hope throughout every interaction. Patients should feel supported during one of the most challenging periods of their recovery, while physiotherapists should feel confident that they are receiving meaningful recovery insights to support clinical decision-making.

---

## Product Philosophy

Every design decision should reinforce these principles:

**Recovery is more than movement.**

**Support patients beyond the clinic.**

**Empower physiotherapists with meaningful Recovery Insights rather than only exercise data.**

**Technology should support people, not replace healthcare professionals.**

---

# Overall Theme

Transform the visual identity into a warm, calming, wellness-inspired healthcare experience.

The interface should feel less like hospital software and more like a thoughtfully designed digital recovery companion.

Design inspiration:

Apple Health

Headspace

Notion

Calm

Modern wellness applications

Avoid harsh clinical interfaces.

Avoid bright saturated colours.

Avoid technology-focused aesthetics.

The platform should feel peaceful, clean, trustworthy, and emotionally supportive.

Visitors should immediately feel:

"This platform understands that recovery is about people, not just movement."

The experience should communicate warmth, trust, calmness, professionalism, and hope while maintaining a clean, modern, and intuitive user experience.
