# Recovr Product Experience & Homepage Transformation

Update the entire Recovr prototype while preserving the existing branding, colour palette (blue-based), workflows, AI rehabilitation monitoring functionality, patient portal, physiotherapist portal, database logic, and overall system architecture.

Do **not** redesign the platform from scratch.

Instead, transform Recovr from a traditional rehabilitation monitoring website into a **human-centred digital recovery platform** that feels warm, welcoming, modern, calming, and supportive.

The platform should no longer feel like a hospital management system.

Instead, it should feel like a trusted recovery companion for patients and a recovery insights platform for physiotherapists.

Every design decision should support one philosophy:

**Recovery doesn't stop when patients leave the clinic.**

**Recovr extends care beyond the clinic by supporting patients throughout recovery while giving physiotherapists complete visibility into the rehabilitation journey.**

---

# Overall Theme & Visual Direction

Maintain the existing blue colour palette but soften the overall visual experience.

The interface should feel:

* Warm
* Calm
* Friendly
* Professional
* Trustworthy
* Minimal
* Spacious
* Modern

Use:

* soft blue gradients
* warm white backgrounds
* rounded cards
* subtle shadows
* smooth spacing
* friendly illustrations
* clean typography

Avoid dark clinical colours or interfaces that resemble hospital management software.

The website should feel approachable for someone recovering from an injury.
